    Before you register

    • Member pricing requires a current FY27 membership with a paid date on or before June 30, 2027 — and you must register with the email tied to your SWE member account. A different email means nonmember pricing.
    • Membership is not included with your registration.
    • Only one promo code per attendee, for both professional and collegiate registration.
    • Need a visa invitation letter? Say so during registration and it is generated automatically, then emailed to you once your registration is confirmed.
    Beware of scams. Cvent is the only authorized registration site for WE26, and Cvent/Passkey is the only official housing partner. SWE does not sell or share attendee or exhibitor contact information, and will never call to book your hotel.

    How to Register

    The registration portal is split into three categories — professional, collegiate, and exhibitor — plus free precollege access to the SWENext Innovation Expo.

    Member pricing eligibility

    To qualify for member rates you need a current individual FY27 membership with a paid date on or before June 30, 2027, and you must register using your SWE member credentials. Registering with an email that isn't attached to your SWE member account will charge you nonmember pricing.

    Attendees without a current membership pay nonmember rates. Nonmembers who use a bulk corporate discount code do not receive FY27 SWE membership with their registration.

    Professional

    All postgraduate attendees, speakers, press, and anyone who is not a collegian or exhibitor — including active-career, retired, unemployed, and STEM reentry attendees.

    • Career fair access: priority access on Friday, Nov. 6, from 10:30–11:30 a.m., then general access.
    • Promo codes: one per attendee.
    • There is no career-fair-only registration for professionals.

    Collegiate

    Undergraduate members and nonmembers, plus full-time graduate students.

    • Career fair access: designated times on Thursday, Nov. 5 and Friday, Nov. 6.
    • Promo codes: one per attendee.
    • There is no career-fair-only registration for collegians.

    Exhibitor

    Booth personnel only, capped at the allotment set by SWE for each booth size.

    • Exhibitor full registration: exhibit hall access at all times plus full WE26 access.
    • Exhibitor floor-only ($150): career fair floor only, no access to the rest of WE26.
    • FY27 membership is not included with either exhibitor registration type.

    Precollege

    Precollege students aged 5–18, parents, and STEM advocates are welcome at the SWENext Innovation Expo. Public registration is free and grants access to the expo only.

    Attendees under 18 are not permitted on the career fair floor unless explicitly permitted by SWE HQ staff and arranged in advance.

    Discounts & Grants

    Six separate ways to pay less for WE26. On the live site these are spread across three pages and a FAQ; here they are one list with their deadlines attached.

    Individual promo code

    Code READYSETGO took 10% off individual registration through Aug. 31. Only one promo code applies per attendee.

    University or college groups

    Groups of 10 or more students prepay for 30% off member and nonmember registration. Orders submitted by Aug. 31 get 30% off early-bird pricing; orders from Sept. 1 – Oct. 1 get 30% off standard pricing. No bulk orders are accepted after Oct. 1.

    SWE emails an invoice within 7–10 business days of your order. Codes are not distributed until payment fully processes, which takes another 5–7 business days. Coordinate your headcount before ordering — changes aren't accommodated and registrations are non-refundable.

    Employers and organizations

    Bringing 25 or more professionals? Prepay for 30% off the nonmember registration rate.

    Faculty and staff

    For every 4 students who register from a university or college, SWE provides 1 free registration for a faculty or staff member. If a faculty member registers before the required student count is confirmed, SWE refunds the fee.

    Middle- and low-income countries

    A limited number of registrations are offered at a flat $100 USD for professionals and $25 USD for collegians who are SWE members living in select countries. The discount applies automatically at checkout. Students from these countries currently studying in the United States are not eligible.

    Member-sponsored registration grants

    SWE members fund a pool of registration grants for other members. Availability and application windows are announced on the official WE26 resources page.

    Stacking rule. Only one promo code applies per attendee, so a bulk group rate and an individual code cannot be combined. The country-based flat rate is applied automatically and is the exception.
