    Before you register

    • Member pricing requires a current FY27 membership with a paid date on or before June 30, 2027 — and you must register with the email tied to your SWE member account. A different email means nonmember pricing.
    • Membership is not included with your registration.
    • Only one promo code per attendee, for both professional and collegiate registration.
    • Need a visa invitation letter? Say so during registration and it is generated automatically, then emailed to you once your registration is confirmed.
    Beware of scams. Cvent is the only authorized registration site for WE26, and Cvent/Passkey is the only official housing partner. SWE does not sell or share attendee or exhibitor contact information, and will never call to book your hotel.

    Member pricing eligibility

    To qualify for member rates you need a current individual FY27 membership with a paid date on or before June 30, 2027, and you must register using your SWE member credentials. Registering with an email that isn't attached to your SWE member account will charge you nonmember pricing.

    Attendees without a current membership pay nonmember rates. Nonmembers who use a bulk corporate discount code do not receive FY27 SWE membership with their registration.

    Professional

    All postgraduate attendees, speakers, press, and anyone who is not a collegian or exhibitor — including active-career, retired, unemployed, and STEM reentry attendees.

    • Career fair access: priority access on Friday, Nov. 6, from 10:30–11:30 a.m., then general access.
    • Promo codes: one per attendee.
    • There is no career-fair-only registration for professionals.

    Collegiate

    Undergraduate members and nonmembers, plus full-time graduate students.

    • Career fair access: designated times on Thursday, Nov. 5 and Friday, Nov. 6.
    • Promo codes: one per attendee.
    • There is no career-fair-only registration for collegians.

    Exhibitor

    Booth personnel only, capped at the allotment set by SWE for each booth size.

    • Exhibitor full registration: exhibit hall access at all times plus full WE26 access.
    • Exhibitor floor-only ($150): career fair floor only, no access to the rest of WE26.
    • FY27 membership is not included with either exhibitor registration type.

    Precollege

    Precollege students aged 5–18, parents, and STEM advocates are welcome at the SWENext Innovation Expo. Public registration is free and grants access to the expo only.

    Attendees under 18 are not permitted on the career fair floor unless explicitly permitted by SWE HQ staff and arranged in advance.

    Individual promo code

    Code READYSETGO took 10% off individual registration through Aug. 31. Only one promo code applies per attendee.

    University or college groups

    Groups of 10 or more students prepay for 30% off member and nonmember registration. Orders submitted by Aug. 31 get 30% off early-bird pricing; orders from Sept. 1 – Oct. 1 get 30% off standard pricing. No bulk orders are accepted after Oct. 1.

    SWE emails an invoice within 7–10 business days of your order. Codes are not distributed until payment fully processes, which takes another 5–7 business days. Coordinate your headcount before ordering — changes aren't accommodated and registrations are non-refundable.

    Employers and organizations

    Bringing 25 or more professionals? Prepay for 30% off the nonmember registration rate.

    Faculty and staff

    For every 4 students who register from a university or college, SWE provides 1 free registration for a faculty or staff member. If a faculty member registers before the required student count is confirmed, SWE refunds the fee.

    Middle- and low-income countries

    A limited number of registrations are offered at a flat $100 USD for professionals and $25 USD for collegians who are SWE members living in select countries. The discount applies automatically at checkout. Students from these countries currently studying in the United States are not eligible.

    Member-sponsored registration grants

    SWE members fund a pool of registration grants for other members. Availability and application windows are announced on the official WE26 resources page.

    Stacking rule. Only one promo code applies per attendee, so a bulk group rate and an individual code cannot be combined. The country-based flat rate is applied automatically and is the exception.

      Affinity Group Lounges

      SWE's affinity groups run lounges where attendees connect around shared identity, career stage, or professional interest — diversity, equity and inclusion groups; career stage groups; and business and interest groups. Sign-up instructions go to leads in July 2026, and all sign-ups route through the appropriate lead. Groups may also run virtual meetups, though simultaneous virtual elements of on-site events are discouraged.

      The venue

      Thomas M. Menino Convention & Exhibition Center
      415 Summer St, Boston, MA 02210

      Registration is in the North Lobby. The career fair runs Nov. 5–6 in the exhibit hall.

      Housing

      Cvent/Passkey is WE26's official housing partner, and the Omni Boston Hotel at the Seaport is the headquarters hotel. Log in to the housing site for available hotels, dates, and rates.

      • Attendees book and manage their own reservations — SWE does not book rooms, and room requests sent directly to SWE are denied.
      • Book inside the block. Reserving outside it can result in penalties to SWE.
      • A credit card is required, but it isn't charged until names are finalized.
      • Hotels may hold a limited number of rooms. If a property shows sold out, contact Cvent/Passkey at housing@swe.org — availability changes often.
      • Shuttle service runs from designated hotels in the block.
      Book early. By late summer, hotel options around SWE events are typically limited. SWE will not prepay accommodations for anyone who doesn't meet an individual hotel's criteria on guest age or accepted credit card.
      Housing scams are common. SWE cannot guarantee rates or availability through any company other than Cvent/Passkey, and does not sell or share attendee contact information. If someone calls offering to book your WE26 room, it isn't SWE.

      Getting around

      • Shuttles run between designated block hotels and the convention center.
      • Mobility scooter rental is available on site and can be reserved in advance.
      • The Westin Boston Seaport (425 Summer St) and Omni Boston Seaport (450 Summer St) are both a short walk from the Menino Center — all three venues sit within the same block of Summer St.

      Accessibility

      SWE provides on-site mobility scooters, an accessible facility, and CART (communication access real-time translation) service on request.

      Deadline: Sept. 15. Accessibility requests must be submitted by Sept. 15 for SWE to guarantee facilitation. This date is easy to miss — it sits inside a FAQ answer on the official site.

      Families and parents

      Lactation rooms

      Private spaces are provided for parents to express milk or feed infants, with comfortable chairs, bottled water, snacks, electrical outlets, and mini-fridges. The convention center also has a Mamava nursing pod — a free-standing private suite with a Bluetooth SmartLock you can reserve, locate, and unlock from the Mamava app. Bring your own pump and accessories for both spaces.

      Child care

      On-site child care for WE26 is arranged through KiddieCorp, registered separately from conference registration.

      Quiet space

      A prayer and meditation room with prayer mats and chairs is open to all attendees.

      Security

      • Firearms and weapons of any kind are banned from the Menino Center for the duration of the event.
      • Entry is permitted only through designated entrances.
      • Everyone entering is subject to security and bag checks. Security canines are present.
      • SWE provides a mix of armed and unarmed security professionals at doors and checkpoints.
      • SWE reserves the right to refuse entry to anyone who does not cooperate with these protocols.

      Visas and invitation letters

      Indicate during registration that you need an invitation letter and one is generated automatically, then emailed to you once your registration is confirmed. Direct all other visa questions to consular or immigration services.

      If your visa application is denied, notify SWE by Oct. 16 and your registration will be refunded.

      14 questions

      Where is WE26 located?
      Venue

      WE26 is held Nov. 5–7, 2026, in person at the Thomas M. Menino Convention & Exhibition Center, 415 Summer St, Boston, MA 02210.

      How do I book a hotel for WE26?
      Housing

      Attendees are responsible for their own accommodations. SWE does not book or manage reservations for attendees, speakers, exhibitors, or guests, and requests sent directly to SWE are denied.

      SWE strongly recommends booking through the Cvent/Passkey hotel block as early as possible. By late summer, hotel options are typically limited.

      What accessibility support is available at WE26?
      Accessibility · Deadline Sept. 15

      SWE provides on-site mobility scooters, an accessible facility, and CART (communication access real-time translation) service on request. Requests must be submitted by Sept. 15 to guarantee facilitation.

      SWE works each year to keep the conference site ADA-compliant to the best of its ability.

      How do I add my résumé to the SWE Career Center?
      Career fair

      Upload your résumé to the SWE Career Center and flag your account as “attending WE26.” Every exhibitor has access to that résumé pool and actively reaches out to people they want to speak with or interview.

      All attendees are strongly encouraged to do this before arriving.

      How does collegiate bulk registration work, and how do we get the 30% discount?
      Registration · Closes Oct. 1

      Purchase 10 or more collegiate registrations for 30% off. No additional codes or discounts apply.

      • Orders through Aug. 31 — 30% off early bird ($275 member / $400 nonmember).
      • Orders Sept. 1 – Oct. 1 — 30% off standard ($350 member / $475 nonmember).
      • No requests accepted after Oct. 1, 2026.

      SWE emails an invoice within 7–10 business days. Codes are not distributed until payment fully processes, which takes another 5–7 business days. Coordinate your headcount first — changes aren't accommodated and registrations are non-refundable.

      Do not submit registration requests for section presidents or faculty advisors; they receive separate instructions.

      What discounts exist for faculty, staff, and section advisors?
      Discounts

      For every 4 students who register from a university or college, SWE provides 1 free registration for a faculty or staff member. If a faculty member registers before the required student count is confirmed, SWE refunds the fee.

      What discounts are available for attendees from middle- and low-income countries?
      Discounts

      SWE offers a limited number of registrations at a flat $100 USD for professionals and $25 USD for collegians who are SWE members living in select countries. The discount applies automatically at checkout.

      Students from these countries currently studying in the United States are not eligible.

      Is membership included with my registration?
      Membership

      No. Membership is not included with your registration.

      How do I get an invitation letter for a travel visa?
      Visas · Refund by Oct. 16

      Indicate during registration that you need one and it is generated automatically, then sent to the email you registered with once your registration is confirmed. Direct all other visa questions to consular or immigration services.

      If your visa application is denied, notify SWE by Oct. 16 and your registration will be refunded.

      Does SWE accommodate dietary restrictions?
      Food

      Special meals aren't available at the sit-down collegiate luncheons, but gluten-free and vegetarian/vegan (dairy-free) options are. SWE collects dietary restriction information for special-event meals. No beef, pork, or seafood is served.

      What can I expect in the lactation room?
      Families

      Safe, clean, private spaces with comfortable chairs, bottled water, and snacks next to electrical outlets. Mini-fridges are provided.

      The convention center also has a Mamava nursing pod — a free-standing private suite with a Bluetooth SmartLock you can reserve, locate, and unlock from the Mamava app. Bring your own pump and accessories for both spaces.

      What will security look like at WE26?
      Safety

      Firearms and weapons of any kind are banned from the Menino Center for the duration of the event, and entry is permitted only through designated entrances.

      Everyone entering is subject to security and bag checks, with security canines present. SWE provides a mix of armed and unarmed security professionals at doors and checkpoints, and reserves the right to refuse entry to anyone who does not cooperate.

      Is there housing for collegiate section presidents?
      Housing

      No. Due to low usage, SWE is not offering collegiate section president housing for WE26.

      What information is available for accepted speakers?
      Speakers

      Accepted speakers who confirm participation receive WE26 speaker resources, including the WE Speaker Toolkit and a WE26 presentation template.

      Exhibiting at WE26

      The WE26 career fair is the world's largest career fair for women and allies in engineering and technology. Exhibitors get access to the SWE Career Center résumé pool, where attendees flag themselves as attending WE26, and can book interview booths that open before the floor does.

      • Prospectus: booth packages, sponsorship tiers, and pricing.
      • Floor plan: the live career fair floor map, updated as booths are claimed.
      • Advertising: separate promotional opportunities across SWE channels.
      • SWENext Innovation Expo booths are registered separately, with their own prospectus.

      Registration for booth personnel

      • Exhibitor full registration — exhibit hall access at all times plus full WE26 access. Limited to the allotment SWE defines for your booth size.
      • Exhibitor floor-only ($150) — career fair floor only, no access to the rest of WE26. Also limited by allotment.
      • FY27 SWE membership is not included with either type, and membership status is not shown on exhibitor badges.
