    About this project

    Why a redesign?

    This concept started with a dead end: on the live site, "Registration" is a dropdown label with no page behind it, so its sub-pages have no hub to return to. The full audit — what's hard to reach today, and what a rebuild buys SWE over the next several conference cycles — is written up alongside the design.
